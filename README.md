## The word

**moist**

## Why this word

It is the one word in English that makes perfectly reasonable people flinch on
hearing it. Nobody has ever said it fondly. Cakes do not need it, weather
reports do not need it, and the rest of us would not miss it for a second.
Deleting it costs the language nothing and spares humanity a small, damp shudder
every time it appears.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

It prints the word above, and nothing else.
